# Beelio Marketing Launch Plan (First 30 Days)

## 1) Goal (Month 1)
- Generate first 20 qualified leads.
- Book 8 sales calls.
- Close 2–3 pilot clients.

## 2) Offer to Sell First
**Core offer:**
- “We set up WhatsApp automation for your business in 7 days.”

**Promise (simple):**
- Faster replies
- Fewer missed bookings
- Less admin work

**Risk reversal:**
- Free workflow audit + demo
- If no fit, no pressure

## 3) Ideal Customer Profile (Start Narrow)
Focus only on 2 niches first:
1. Clinics
2. Salons / spas

Regions to start:
- Uganda
- Kenya

Decision makers:
- Owner
- Operations manager

## 4) Funnel (Simple)
1. Content + outbound outreach
2. Click to WhatsApp or booking link
3. 15-min discovery call
4. Custom demo
5. Paid pilot + onboarding

## 5) Channels to Activate Now
1. WhatsApp outreach (warm + referral)
2. LinkedIn founder content
3. Local Facebook business groups
4. Short-form video (TikTok/Reels)
5. Partner referrals (agencies, consultants)

## 6) Weekly Execution Cadence
### Monday
- Publish 1 case-study style post
- Send 30 targeted outreach messages

### Tuesday
- Publish 1 short video demo
- Follow up all open leads

### Wednesday
- Publish 1 educational post (“how to reduce missed bookings”)
- Send 30 more outreach messages

### Thursday
- Host 20-min live demo (Zoom/Google Meet)
- Share replay clips

### Friday
- Publish 1 customer story/testimonial
- Pipeline review + next-week plan

## 7) Copy Pack (Ready to Use)

### A) LinkedIn Post Template
**Hook:** Still missing customers because nobody replies fast on WhatsApp?

Most service businesses lose leads after-hours.

We help clinics and salons automate WhatsApp replies, bookings, and reminders in 7 days.

If you want fewer missed appointments and less admin work, message “DEMO”.

### B) WhatsApp Outreach Template
Hi [Name], quick one:

We help [clinics/salons] automate WhatsApp replies + booking reminders so they stop losing leads after-hours.

Would you like a free 10-minute workflow audit for your business this week?

### C) Follow-up (48 hours)
Hi [Name], just checking in.

### D) Call Booking CTA
“Book a free demo — we’ll map your workflow and show how to automate replies, bookings, and reminders.”

## 8) Landing Page Messaging (Use Everywhere)
- Headline: **Get more bookings from WhatsApp in 7 days**
- Subheadline: **We set up automated replies, booking flows, and reminders so you stop losing customers and save hours each week.**
- CTA 1: **Book Free Demo**
- CTA 2: **See How It Works**

## 9) Lead Tracking (Simple CRM)
Use a sheet with columns:
- Name
- Business
- Channel
- Status (New / Contacted / Replied / Call Booked / Closed)
- Next action date
- Notes

Target weekly numbers:
- 150 outreaches
- 20 replies
- 8 calls booked
- 2 closed deals

## 10) 14-Day Sprint (Do This First)
### Days 1–3
- Finalize one-page offer
- Publish 2 posts
- Send 60 outreach messages

### Days 4–7
- Run first demo calls
- Post 2 short videos
- Send follow-ups

### Days 8–10
- Publish one mini case study
- Send 60 more outreach messages

### Days 11–14
- Close first pilot clients
- Collect testimonials
- Turn wins into next content cycle

- Reply rate > 10%
- Call booking rate > 4%
- Close rate from calls > 20%
- CAC trend down week-over-week
